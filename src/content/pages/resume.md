Engineering leader across applied AI, API platforms, real-time payments and advertising infrastructure. Author of two books on API design and cloud infrastructure, and founder of AIP.dev, Google's public API design guidance.

## Experience

### Director, Applied AI

**Google DeepMind** · Singapore · Jan 2026 - present

- Push the technical boundaries of what Google DeepMind's models can do, building work that stays 6+ months ahead of their out-of-the-box capabilities.
- Work across the full range of DeepMind models, including Gemini, Gemma, WeatherNext and MetNet.

### Principal Software Engineer

**Meta** · Singapore · Feb 2023 - Jan 2026

- **Technical advisor to the COO.** Worked with COO Javi Olivan on speculative, skunkworks AI projects in Singapore exploring new Asia-focused business lines, and supported technical due diligence for M&A.
- **Ad Signal.** Worked on Meta Pixel and other inference-focused data, turning patterns in user behavior into signals for ad targeting.
- **WhatsApp Business.** Explored AI adoption for businesses on WhatsApp, and worked on WhatsApp Flows, advanced interaction routines, and developer tools and SDKs.

### Principal Software Engineer, Payments

**Google** · Singapore · Jan 2020 - Feb 2023

- Led Google's work on Mojaloop, the open-source real-time payment system, including its third-party payment initiation integration.
- Represented Google on the Technical Governing Board of the Mojaloop Foundation.
- Advised governments, central banks and regulators on real-time payment systems, including FedNow (US), NPP (Australia), Pix (Brazil) and IPN (Egypt).
- Advised Google Pay on API design and third-party integrations in new markets, including Japan and Brazil.

### Principal Software Engineer, Google Cloud

**Google** · New York · Dec 2013 - Jan 2020

- Led API design across Google and Alphabet, and founded AIP.dev (API Improvement Proposals) to make those design rules public.
- Designed and built client libraries for Google Cloud APIs in Python, Java, Node.js, Ruby, PHP, C++ and Go.
- Audited and improved the usability of Google Cloud's APIs.

### Software Engineering Manager, Display Advertising

**Google** · New York · Jun 2010 - Dec 2013

- Led the migration of Invite Media's Bid Manager from Amazon EC2 to Google's infrastructure, relaunched as DoubleClick Bid Manager, keeping the system stable while traffic and data volumes kept growing.
- Led east coast engineering for DoubleClick Bid Manager and worked directly with clients in the US and Europe.

### Vice President, Engineering

**Invite Media** · Philadelphia · Jan 2008 - Jun 2010

- Built and managed the engineering team, split between Philadelphia and New York, behind Bid Manager: the first "universal buying platform" for display advertising, bidding in real time across multiple ad exchanges.
- The ad serving system handled hundreds of thousands of requests per second across several thousand virtual machines and processed several terabytes of data a day.
- Acquired by Google in 2010.
