A lot of readers asked for solutions to the exercises in [*API Design Patterns*](https://apidesignbook.com), so here they are, free for everyone.

Each one goes further than the answer. I walk through the trade-offs between the possible designs, so you can see why a particular solution is right, or why several of them are. Have a go at the exercise first, then open the solution.
